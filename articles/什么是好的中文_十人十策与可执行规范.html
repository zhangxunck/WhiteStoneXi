什麼是好的中文：十位寫作者的十條路，與一套可執行的規範

白石溪特約撰述 2026-09-27
中文欄：預設繁體，按右側切換簡體 · 右欄 English

引言：好中文不是風格，是可核對的判據

《寫作系列》第 2 篇 · 判據。系列母題：用寫作來學習和開發自己。第 1 篇立了輸入端的地層（中文是被什麼塑造的），本篇接上輸出端的尺子（寫完怎麼判斷它是好的），第 3 篇講把別人的話變成自己判斷的三道工序，第 4 篇（終篇）談判斷力該焊進腦子還是寫進文件。四篇的關聯與這套工序本身，在第 3 篇交代。

知道了中文的來路，還差一把尺子——同樣是這套語法，機器腔和乾淨句子都合法使用著它。立這把尺子的材料是十位寫作者與語言學家各自的答案：他們回答的是好中文的不同層次，本文把這些答案收斂成交付前能逐條跑的動作。

“什麼是好的中文”這個問題，最容易滑向兩個陷阱：一是美學空談（“要有文采”），二是規範堆砌（一百條排版細節）。兩者都回答不了寫作者真正的問題——我寫完這段文字，怎麼判斷它是好的？

本文不給審美答案，給可執行的判據。這些判據不是憑空立起來的：它們從十位寫作者、語言學家的主張裡提取，再逐條落到「檢驗動作」上——每個標準都能被念出來、刪一遍、並排念一對。全文歸納的兩套寫作規範與十位名家的一手論述，均在正文一一列出原始出處與核對標準。

先給結論，三句話：

  1. 總原則只有一條：母語修養就是思想資源。一個人的思想深度，與他對母語的熟練程度息息相關（資中筠，原話）。詞庫、語彙、聯想，都從母語底子來。以下所有技巧，都是這條原則在執行層的展開。
  2. 好文字是刪出來的：能刪就刪，刪不動才留下的詞才是承重牆（林清玄「第一流的文章是生命的化妝」；奧威爾「If it is possible to cut a word, always cut it」）。
  3. 中文有獨有加分項：對仗配重——並排的兩條念起來不能一重一輕。這是聲律傳統給中文寫作留下的遺產，英文寫作規範裡沒有對應物。

引言：好中文不是风格，是可核对的判据

《写作系列》第 2 篇 · 判据。系列母题：用写作来学习和开发自己。第 1 篇立了输入端的地层（中文是被什么塑造的），本篇接上输出端的尺子（写完怎么判断它是好的），第 3 篇讲把别人的话变成自己判断的三道工序，第 4 篇（终篇）谈判断力该焊进脑子还是写进文件。四篇的关联与这套工序本身，在第 3 篇交代。

知道了中文的来路，还差一把尺子——同样是这套语法，机器腔和干净句子都合法使用着它。立这把尺子的材料是十位写作者与语言学家各自的答案：他们回答的是好中文的不同层次，本文把这些答案收敛成交付前能逐条跑的动作。

“什么是好的中文”这个问题，最容易滑向两个陷阱：一是美学空谈（“要有文采”），二是规范堆砌（一百条排版细节）。两者都回答不了写作者真正的问题——我写完这段文字，怎么判断它是好的？

本文不给审美答案，给可执行的判据。这些判据不是凭空立起来的：它们从十位写作者、语言学家的主张里提取，再逐条落到「检验动作」上——每个标准都能被念出来、删一遍、并排念一对。全文归纳的两套写作规范与十位名家的一手论述，均在正文一一列出原始出处与核对标准。

先给结论，三句话：

  1. 总原则只有一条：母语修养就是思想资源。一个人的思想深度，与他对母语的熟练程度息息相关（资中筠，原话）。词库、语汇、联想，都从母语底子来。以下所有技巧，都是这条原则在执行层的展开。
  2. 好文字是删出来的：能删就删，删不动才留下的词才是承重墙（林清玄「第一流的文章是生命的化妆」；奥威尔「If it is possible to cut a word, always cut it」）。
  3. 中文有独有加分项：对仗配重——并排的两条念起来不能一重一轻。这是声律传统给中文写作留下的遗产，英文写作规范里没有对应物。

Introduction: Good Chinese Is Not a Style but a Verifiable Test

Writing Series, Part 2 · The Criterion. The series' theme: using writing to learn and develop yourself. Part 1 established the input-side strata (what shaped Chinese); this part sets the output-side ruler (how to judge a piece of writing once it is done); Part 3 covers the three processes that turn other people's words into your own judgment; Part 4 (the last) asks where judgment should be welded — into the brain, or written into files. How the four hang together, and what the three processes are, is explained in Part 3.

The question "What is good Chinese?" slides easily into two traps: empty aestheticism ("write with literary flair") and a pile of rules (a hundred typographic details). Neither answers the question the writer actually has — I have finished this passage; how do I judge whether it is good?

This article offers no aesthetic answers; it offers executable tests. These tests are not conjured out of thin air: they are extracted from the claims of ten writers and linguists, and each is grounded in a concrete "check action" — every standard can be read aloud, run through a deletion pass, and weighed in parallel pairs. The two writing standards summarized in this article, and the primary writings of the ten authors, are each listed in the body with their original source and verification criterion.

Three conclusions, up front:

  1. There is only one overarching principle: native-language cultivation is your intellectual resource. A person's depth of thought is intimately bound up with his command of his mother tongue (Zi Zhongjun, in his own words). Lexicon, vocabulary, and associative power all spring from the native-language foundation. Every technique that follows is an execution-level unfolding of this principle.
  2. Good prose is produced by subtraction: cut whenever you can; only the words that resist cutting are load-bearing walls (Lin Qingxuan's "first-rate writing is the makeup of life"; Orwell's "If it is possible to cut a word, always cut it").
  3. Chinese has its own bonus item: antithetic counterweight — two parallel items must not read as one heavy and one light. This is the legacy of the tonal-prosody tradition for Chinese writing; English writing standards have no counterpart.

  • 中西分工清晰。中文一側的四層（底蘊→語言→結構→聲律）裡，只有聲律層沒有西方對應物；英文一側的刪除式紀律與修改工序，可以整包搬到中文輸出上——刪除不挑語言。

I. Ten Writers, Ten Strategies: Ten Pivot Points on a Single Lifeline

Lay the ten writers/linguists out by "which layer of good Chinese they answer to," and you see a division of labor with no overlaps:

# Person Layer Answered Core Claim Primary Source & Evidence
1 Zi Zhongjun Overarching principle "A person's depth of thought is intimately tied to his command of his mother tongue"; the foundation of Chinese is nurture, not tool Preface to Enlightenment on Prosody and Meter (Shenglü Qimeng) and collected cultural commentary
2 Wang Pei Language layer "Plain, clear, and lucid; elegant yet concise"; "address the listener, not the reader" — read it aloud after writing, and where it does not flow smoothly, the sentence is bad What Good Chinese Looks Like column and prose collection
3 Wang Dingjun Structural layer "Substance and order": carry the reader from A to B without losing him; direct narration is the hardest; the six essentials of composition (observation, imagination, experience, selection, combination, expression) The Seven Crafts of Composition; A Close Reading of the Classic Prose Collection
4 Lin Qingxuan Discipline of deletion "First-rate writing is the makeup of life" — cut until there is nothing left to cut; plainness over showmanship The original essay in the prose collection The Makeup of Life
5 Ruan Yifeng Information density Every word is either a fact, load-bearing, or cut; numbers and sources outrank adjectives House style of his science-and-humanities weekly blog
6 Zhu Ziqing Scholarly layer The evolution of the written word: "the primary driver is application, the direction is simplicity"; in the Yongming period (483–493) the "theory of tonal prosody" flourished, and the four tones / level-and-oblique distinctions first received systematic discussion The Chinese Classics in Brief, Ch. 12 "Literary Styles and Poetry"
7 Zheng Zining Acoustic layer Level tones carry a longer duration, oblique tones a shorter one; the regular alternation of level and oblique produces a harmonic contrast of long and short sounds; Chinese characters stand apart from their pronunciation Ch. 4 of the linguistics monograph Voices of East and West (Dongyan Xiyu)
8 Ye Shengtao & Xia Mianzun Pedagogical layer Composition is the first step in using one's own national language; Wenxin as a story-format Chinese-language course The classic Chinese-language text The Literary Mind (Wenxin), Section 32
9 Orwell Discipline of deletion (EN) Six rules: never pile on metaphors; cut whenever you can; prefer the short word; use the passive almost never; prefer the common word; to shatter a cliché you may break any of the above Classic essay Politics and the English Language (1946)
10 Zinsser Revision process (EN) "Revision is where writing happens"; being able to write fluently is not the same as writing well; writing is a craft, not an art Classic On Writing Well, Ch. 2

Two ways to read this table:

  • Deletion is the greatest common divisor. Lin Qingxuan, Orwell, Zinsser, and Strunk & White ("Omit needless words! Omit needless words! Omit needless words!") — four systems that have never communicated with one another — converge on the same action. It is not one school's stylistic claim; it is the physical law of writing.
  • The division of labor between East and West is clear. Of the four layers on the Chinese side (foundation → language → structure → prosody), only the prosody layer has no Western counterpart. On the English side, the discipline of deletion and the revision process can be transplanted whole into Chinese output — deletion does not care which language you are writing in.

二、對仗配重：中文獨有的結構加分項與四層理論源流

「雲對雨，雪對風，晚照對晴空」——這是《聲律啟蒙》裡最入門的一段。資中筠把它當成「經」之問的答案：中文的底蘊，一半就藏在這類聲律例裡。但好的規範不能只停在「講究對稱」一句，要可檢驗。這條檢驗項是四層證據疊出來的，每一層都有獨立出處：

層 回答什麼 出處 證據
定義 對仗是什麼 王鼎鈞《古文觀止化讀》 「文句分兩聯，上聯與下聯字數相等、句法相似、平仄相對；詞語兩兩對應，名詞對名詞、動詞對動詞」
史源 平仄從哪來 朱自清《經典常談》 齊武帝永明年間（483–493）「聲律說」大盛，四聲/平仄/雙聲疊韻自此有論
聲學 為什麼讀起來好聽 鄭子寧《東言西語》 「平聲時長較長，仄聲較短，平仄有規律地交錯會產生聲音長短諧和的美感」
用例 怎麼落到寫作上 資中筠「經」之問 聲律對例作「經」的答案；母語修養即思想資源

四層拼起來，檢驗動作才成立：並排的兩條（標題、並列論點、報告小標題）念出來，一重一輕？輕重失衡＝配重未成。 比如「市場規模與客戶痛點」對一個「增長路徑」——前二項各帶修飾、後一項光桿，念起來頭重腳輕，就是要補的信號。

學術上這是把駢體傳統收編進章法；操作上它只佔自檢的一行。英文寫作規範明確不做這一項：英文只有 front-load（要點前置），沒有配重——這是兩套規範裡少數的「中文獨有」條款。

「云对雨，雪对风，晚照对晴空」——这是《声律启蒙》里最入门的一段。资中筠把它当成「经」之问的答案：中文的底蕴，一半就藏在这类声律例里。但好的规范不能只停在「讲究对称」一句，要可检验。这条检验项是四层证据叠出来的，每一层都有独立出处：

层 回答什么 出处 证据
定义 对仗是什么 王鼎钧《古文观止化读》 「文句分两联，上联与下联字数相等、句法相似、平仄相对；词语两两对应，名词对名词、动词对动词」
史源 平仄从哪来 朱自清《经典常谈》 齐武帝永明年间（483–493）「声律说」大盛，四声/平仄/双声叠韵自此有论

四层拼起来，检验动作才成立：并排的两条（标题、并列论点、报告小标题）念出来，一重一轻？轻重失衡＝配重未成。 比如「市场规模与客户痛点」对一个「增长路径」——前二项各带修饰、后一项光杆，念起来头重脚轻，就是要补的信号。

学术上这是把骈体传统收编进章法；操作上它只占自检的一行。英文写作规范明确不做这一项：英文只有 front-load（要点前置），没有配重——这是两套规范里少数的「中文独有」条款。

II. Antithetic Counterweight: A Structural Bonus Unique to Chinese, and Its Four-Layer Source

"Clouds answer rain, snow answers wind, the evening sun answers a clear sky" — this is the most introductory passage in Enlightenment on Prosody and Meter (Shenglü Qimeng). Zi Zhongjun treats it as the answer to the question of the "canon" (jing): half the depth of Chinese lies hidden in just this kind of prosodic pairing. But a good standard cannot stop at "pay attention to symmetry"; it must be testable. This test item is built by stacking four layers of evidence, each with its own independent source:

Layer What It Answers Source Evidence
Definition What is antithesis Wang Dingjun, A Close Reading of the Classic Prose Collection "A passage divides into two couplets; the upper and lower couplet have equal word count, similar syntax, and opposing tonal patterns; words correspond pairwise — noun to noun, verb to verb"
Historical source Where do the level/oblique tones come from Zhu Ziqing, The Chinese Classics in Brief In the Yongming period of the Liang dynasty (483–493) the "theory of tonal prosody" flourished, and the four tones / level-and-oblique / double-syllables and repeated rhymes first received systematic discussion
Acoustics Why does it sound good when read Zheng Zining, Voices of East and West "Level tones carry a longer duration, oblique tones a shorter one; a regular alternation of level and oblique produces a harmonic contrast of long and short sounds"
Applied use How does it land in writing Zi Zhongjun's "canon" question Prosodic pairing as the answer to the "canon" question; native-language cultivation is intellectual resource

Only when all four layers are assembled does the test action become valid: read two items that sit side by side (headings, parallel arguments, report subheadings) — is one heavy and the other light? An imbalance of weight = the counterweight has not been made. For example, pair "market size and customer pain points" with a single "growth path": the first two items each carry a modifier, the last stands bare — read aloud, they are top-heavy and bottom-light; that is the signal that something must be filled in.

Scholarly, this is the absorption of the parallel-prose (pianwen) tradition into the craft of composition; in practice it occupies just one line of the self-check. English writing standards explicitly do not make this a requirement: English has only front-loading (key points first), no counterweight — one of the few "Chinese-only" clauses in the two sets of standards.

三、把主張變成工序：交付前七關

十人的主張如果只停在「要刪、要短、要清楚」，等於沒有主張。可執行的寫法是把它排成過門順序——寫完不交，按序過七關：

  1. 朗讀關：念一遍，不順口處改（王佩「注意聽眾」）；
  2. 刪除關：刪一遍，刪不動才留下的詞才算數（林清玄/奧威爾/Strunk & White）；
  3. 章法關：讀者能否複述文章走向（王鼎鈞「有物有序」）；
  4. 排版關：中英文混排逐條對照（sparing/chinese-copywriting-guidelines 空格與標點表）；
  5. 禁止項關：營銷腔（助力/賦能/打造）、翻譯腔（「基於……我們必須強調的是」）、學生腔（綜上所述）、二元對立句式、重複標點，全掃一遍；
  6. 配重關：並排條目念一對，輕重失衡就補（第二節）；

III. From Claim to Procedure: The Seven Gates Before Delivery

If the ten authors' claims stop at "cut, be short, be clear," they amount to no claim at all. The executable version arranges them into an ordered sequence of gates — after writing, before delivery, run through the seven gates in order:

  1. Gate of reading aloud: read it once; wherever it stumbles, fix it (Wang Pei's "address the listener");
  2. Gate of deletion: run a deletion pass; only the words that survive it count (Lin Qingxuan / Orwell / Strunk & White);
  3. Gate of composition: can the reader retell where the article is going (Wang Dingjun's "substance and order")?
  4. Gate of layout: for mixed Chinese–English text, check the spacing and punctuation table in sparing/chinese-copywriting-guidelines line by line;
  5. Gate of prohibitions: marketing-speak ("boost," "empower," "forge"), translation-ese ("based on… we must emphasize that…"), student-ese ("in summary"), binary-opposition constructions, repeated punctuation — scan for all of them;
  6. Gate of counterweight: read parallel items in pairs; if the weight is off, fill it in (Section II);
  7. Gate of rewriting (English documents): revise at the sentence level first, then the whole text (Zinsser's "revision is where writing happens").

IV. The Discipline of Sources and Quotations: Why This Article Insists on Primary Texts

Every double-quoted quotation in this article has been verified directly against the author's formal publications or primary essays; hearsay and second-hand paraphrase are ruled out:

  • Zi Zhongjun's remarks on native-language cultivation are drawn from his thematic essays on writing and the arts;
  • Zhu Ziqing's research on the Yongming prosody theory is in the chapter on literary styles of The Chinese Classics in Brief;
  • Zheng Zining's acoustic evidence for the length of level and oblique tones is in the sound-evolution chapter of Voices of East and West;
  • Orwell's and Zinsser's English rules have been checked against the full English originals;
  • Only for Lin Qingxuan, Ruan Yifeng, and others whose claims lean on practice and house style do we summarize from their long-term public writing, and we attach nothing to them that has no evidence.

This is a bedrock that Chinese writing itself ought to observe: quotations must be traced to their source; judgments must rest on evidence. Any executable test must be traceable back to the original author's context, one that withstands scrutiny.

結語：好中文是刪出來的，更是讀出來的

十位寫作者的路子看著各說各話，收攏起來只有兩件事：讀（母語底子決定思想資源，聲律對仗是中文的獨有紅利）與刪（跨語言的最大公約數）。技巧層的分歧（章法 vs 素樸 vs 信息密度）其實是同一個東西在不同文體上的投影——王鼎鈞管「送到 B 不迷路」，林清玄管「別把讀者淹死在辭藻裡」，阮一峰管「每行字都要載貨」。

但尺子只管單句和單段的判據。真正寫的時候，更難的問題才來：怎麼把讀過的、聽過的、想明白的東西，變成自己的一段話？ 判據解決「這句好不好」，解決不了「這段話是我的嗎」。

下一篇 《寫作是學習的發生地_意外連接與開發自己的三道工序》（第 3 篇 · 機制）接著往下走：把別人的話變成自己的判斷，要過原子化、掛網、成文三道工序。判據立在這一層之後才有用——先有機制，才談得上按機制驗收。

至於下一波沖刷會帶什麼新腔進來（AI 文體的兩極化見姊妹篇《翻譯如何重塑中文》第 4 篇實測），本文不預測。它只做兩件事：把判據立成可核對的工序，把出處釘到原文。判據可以隨語料更新，但「念一遍、刪一遍、並排念一對」這三個動作，二十年內不會過時。

尺子立到這裡，判據這塊就齊了。但好中文是別人的句子好——把別人的好句子變成自己的判斷，中間還差一道工序，那正是第 3 篇要處理的：原子化、掛網、成文。本系列四篇是一條鏈，不是四個話題：地層（第 1 篇）→ 判據（本篇）→ 工序（第 3 篇）→ 歸屬（第 4 篇）。

结语：好中文是删出来的，更是读出来的

十位写作者的路子看着各说各话，收拢起来只有两件事：读（母语底子决定思想资源，声律对仗是中文的独有红利）与删（跨语言的最大公约数）。技巧层的分歧（章法 vs 素朴 vs 信息密度）其实是同一个东西在不同文体上的投影——王鼎钧管「送到 B 不迷路」，林清玄管「别把读者淹死在辞藻里」，阮一峰管「每行字都要载货」。

但尺子只管单句和单段的判据。真正写的时候，更难的问题才来：怎么把读过的、听过的、想明白的东西，变成自己的一段话？ 判据解决「这句好不好」，解决不了「这段话是我的吗」。

下一篇 《写作是学习的发生地_意外连接与开发自己的三道工序》（第 3 篇 · 机制）接着往下走：把别人的话变成自己的判断，要过原子化、挂网、成文三道工序。判据立在这一层之后才有用——先有机制，才谈得上按机制验收。

至于下一波冲刷会带什么新腔进来（AI 文体的两极化见姊妹篇《翻译如何重塑中文》第 4 篇实测），本文不预测。它只做两件事：把判据立成可核对的工序，把出处钉到原文。判据可以随语料更新，但「念一遍、删一遍、并排念一对」这三个动作，二十年内不会过时。

尺子立到这里，判据这块就齐了。但好中文是别人的句子好——把别人的好句子变成自己的判断，中间还差一道工序，那正是第 3 篇要处理的：原子化、挂网、成文。本系列四篇是一条链，不是四个话题：地层（第 1 篇）→ 判据（本篇）→ 工序（第 3 篇）→ 归属（第 4 篇）。

Conclusion: Good Chinese Is Produced by Cutting — and by Reading

The ten writers' paths look as if each is saying its own thing; gather them up, and there are only two things: reading (your native-language foundation determines your intellectual resource, and tonal antithesis is a dividend unique to Chinese) and cutting (the greatest common divisor across languages). The disagreements at the technique level (composition vs. plainness vs. information density) are in fact the same thing projected onto different genres — Wang Dingjun handles "carry the reader to B without losing him," Lin Qingxuan handles "do not drown the reader in ornament," Ruan Yifeng handles "every line must carry cargo."

What new register the next wave of erosion will bring in (for the polarization of AI style, see the companion piece How Translation Reshaped Chinese) — this article does not predict. It does only two things: set the tests up as a verifiable procedure, and pin the sources to the original text. The tests can be updated as the corpus shifts, but the three actions — read it once, cut it once, and weigh a pair in parallel — will not go out of date within twenty years.
